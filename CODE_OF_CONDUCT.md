# Lumi Code of Conduct

Lumi adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/)
as the baseline for participation in this project.

## Our standards

Everyone participating in Lumi is expected to:

- engage kindly, honestly and professionally;
- respect different viewpoints, experiences and levels of expertise;
- give and accept constructive feedback;
- take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm;
- keep technical disagreement focused on the work rather than the person;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and unwanted
  attention.

These expectations apply to Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, code review,
commit messages, official community channels and public interactions where
someone is representing Lumi.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `tech@victorblan.co`. Include only the
information needed to understand the incident. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible and reviewed fairly without retaliation
against a good-faith reporter.

For an immediate threat, contact the appropriate local authorities or platform
safety team first.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may clarify expectations, remove or edit content, reject a
contribution, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or
permanently ban a participant. The response will be proportionate to the
behavior, its impact and any pattern of earlier conduct.
