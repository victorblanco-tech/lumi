# Lumi brand assets

Status: **approved product mark**

The Lumi mark combines an RGB audio waveform with a cyan light fan and two
thin laser beams. It represents Lumi taking over the combined timing work of a
sound and lighting engineer.

- `lumi-logo-approved-concept.png` is the approved composition sheet retained
  as design evidence;
- `lumi-mark-approved.png` is the deterministic square crop used to derive the
  macOS app icon and the persistent in-app navigation mark;
- `LumiWordmark.imageset` is the transparent, deterministic crop of the
  approved horizontal color wordmark used by the expanded navigation;
- generated app assets live in `apps/macos/Lumi/Resources/Assets.xcassets`.

Do not redraw, recolor or change the relative waveform/light geometry without
a new explicit brand decision. The Lumi and VB Tech names and visual identity
remain covered by `TRADEMARKS.md`.
