# Lumi 0.6.4-dev-35 — retain logical cue completion

Dev-34 hardware acceptance exposed duplicate buildup commands under different
MIDI generations, within the same transport epoch, load, plan revision and
phrase. Forecasting the next cue replaced the executor's single state slot;
the delayed current-phrase notification could then request the completed cue
again and cancel the next forecast.

The executor now keeps a separate completed-through watermark. Scheduling or
cancelling unsent work cannot erase it. A completed or earlier phrase in that
same context is rejected without mutating the pending next cue. A new execution
epoch (Start, confirmed seek/loop re-entry) clears it; another load, deck or plan
revision is a distinct identity. Only confirmed emission advances completion.
Failed/cancelled unsent work remains retryable. Storage and lookup are constant
size, with no extra I/O or SoundSwitch timeline manipulation.

The regression reproduces the hardware ordering (phrase 12 sent, phrase 13
forecast, phrase 12 notification delayed). It failed before the fix and must
leave the future cue untouched after the fix. Tests also cover cancellation,
failure/retry, plan revision and new-epoch re-entry.

This does not fix or explain the independently observed gaps in received CDJ
beat/status messages. Physical light beat alignment is not inferred from MIDI
deadline measurements. Hardware/UI acceptance is pending below.
