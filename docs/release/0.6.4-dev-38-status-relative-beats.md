# Dev-38 — prevent stale bar beats from manufacturing a lead

## Scope

This addresses a reproduced provider bug, not the still-open upstream receive
latency. No waveform, MIDI mappings, output offset, Link phase, or automatic
SoundSwitch correction is changed.

The previous beat-to-position calculation chose a bar around the maximum of
the latest absolute status and our own previous position plus one. A late bar
beat could consequently be assigned to the following bar. Subsequent events
could preserve or accumulate this false lead. In the saved real session,
canonical position was 8–11 beats ahead of the latest received status; status
itself can also be late, so that difference is not a physical audio measurement.

## Change and regression

When absolute status is less than one beat old in the bridge clock, use its
nearest bar boundary instead of promoting the reference from our own position.
Older boundaries and already-accepted duplicate boundaries do not refresh the
timeline anchor. Confirmed seeks keep their explicit separate path. Sparse
status still falls back to exact-beat progression after the one-beat window.

A deterministic reordered-bar regression failed on the original code at
800 ms: position 104 instead of at most 102. It passes after the change, as do
the other 21 provider integration tests. Two pre-existing fixtures were made
temporally meaningful: the sparse-status test now emits beats at 155 BPM rather
than 100 Hz, and freshness recovery uses a new beat and receive timestamp,
not an exact replay of the old packet. No assertion was removed.

## Receive-path isolation

Bounded diagnostic probes run without the engine, MIDI, USB reads, or player
controls. They use normal discovery/session announcements and release sockets
before another receiver starts. The initial 45-second raw receiver recorded
1,500 monotone Player 1 positions; a 45-second Beat Link-only receiver recorded
1,501. These short observations do not exonerate either layer.

The complete bridge run independently with the packaged JVM recorded 1,616
positions with the expected loop wrap and two smaller backwards steps. An
IPv4-only comparison also reproduced backwards steps; that JVM option is NOT
included in the product. A longer raw comparison and desktop acceptance are
pending. Do not describe this build as a complete timing fix or show-ready.
