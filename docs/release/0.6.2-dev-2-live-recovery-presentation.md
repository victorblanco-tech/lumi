# Lumi `0.6.2-dev-2`: Live recovery presentation

## Outcome

Live now returns to green as soon as the current Master has recovered with a
valid exact Light Plan. A newly joined, unmatched or still-loading second
Player remains visibly `AUTO HELD` on its own card but no longer keeps the
whole show in a stale Phrase warning.

The fail-safe boundary is unchanged: when the Master itself has no trusted
plan, Lumi continues to show the global hold warning and does not invent a
lighting decision.

## Isolation

This change is presentation-only. It does not write to or restart Pro DJ Link,
the exactly-once AutoLoop executor, Ableton Link, Light Output or Lumi Remote.
Those realtime lanes remain owned by the engine and continue independently of
the macOS client lifecycle.

## Evidence

- 58 `LumiLiveWorkspace` tests pass, including separate recovered-Master,
  idle-held and held-Master cases;
- the complete Rust workspace builds at `0.6.2-dev-2`;
- version, structure, architecture and documentation gates pass.
