# 0.4.0-dev-46 — Independent position authority

## Physical finding

Dev-45 separated the macOS presentation clock from show output, but its
three-packet position consensus was still insufficient. During a physical
CDJ-1500X long-loop run, the AutoLoop MIDI lane stayed healthy at p95 0.1 ms
with zero late dispatches or saturation, while Pro DJ Link still accepted
several false transport discontinuities and hard Link re-anchors. This proved
that the fault was upstream of CoreMIDI and independent from app foregrounding.

## Decision

- Track-time continuity is projected with `effective BPM / original BPM`.
  CDJ pitch therefore cannot masquerade as a forward seek after coalescing or
  temporary scheduling pressure.
- A millisecond jump must also move more than two locally mapped beats before
  it can become a transport discontinuity.
- An arbitrary seek, beatjump or loop wrap must be corroborated by the latest
  independent `CdjStatus` absolute beat and three coherent precise packets.
- An imported Hot Cue target uses two coherent precise packets and does not
  wait for the slower status cadence.
- Until confirmation, the old authoritative generation remains active and no
  phrase, AutoLoop or Link state is changed.

## Automated acceptance

- provider tests cover pitched playback, stale interleaving, uncorroborated
  position clusters, status-corroborated jumps and the known-Hot-Cue fast path;
- the exact-position output barrier remains mandatory before any future MIDI
  deadline can be released;
- LiveWorkspace tests retain presentation-only resume after app activation.

## Physical acceptance

Validated on 17 August 2026 with Player 1 in the physical CDJ-1500X long loop,
Player 2 paused and SoundSwitch connected through both Lumi Virtual MIDI and
Ableton Link:

- during a sampled 35-second interval, the observed CDJ beat crossed the loop
  boundary once (`176 -> 64`); confirmed discontinuities advanced exactly once
  (`5 -> 6`) and hard corrections advanced exactly once (`8 -> 9`);
- during a subsequent complete loop with SoundSwitch kept in the foreground,
  confirmed discontinuities again advanced exactly once (`6 -> 7`) and hard
  corrections exactly once (`9 -> 10`); app foregrounding added no correction;
- AutoLoop realtime output stayed empty between commands, peaked at three
  queued commands, measured p95 `0.1 ms`, and recorded zero late dispatches and
  zero saturation;
- Pro DJ Link ingress recorded zero critical saturation and the provider made
  zero automatic restarts;
- SoundSwitch remained responsive after the soak and reported Link `155.0 BPM`
  with Control One connected.

The imported-Hot-Cue fast path remains covered by automated regression tests;
no physical Hot Cue button press was part of this acceptance run.
