# `0.4.0-dev-42` stable Pro DJ Link transport and passive media access

## Goal

Keep the show-critical Pro DJ Link input focused on device, transport and beat
facts, reject false transport discontinuities and leave no Ableton Link peer
behind when Lumi exits.

## Change

- The bridge no longer starts Beat Link's active metadata/signature query
  chain.
- Track identity remains deterministic through source player, source slot and
  Rekordbox ID from deck status.
- Track metadata, waveform, beat grid, hot cues and phrases continue to come
  from Lumi's trusted read-only USB mirror.
- Beat events, play/pause, master changes, seek/hotcue discontinuities and
  effective BPM remain unchanged.
- Playing deck-status progress is validated against elapsed time and effective
  BPM. It can advance an internal baseline after missed Beat packets, but it
  cannot emit a beat boundary or rewind a newer precise Beat observation.
- Only a position change outside that elapsed-time envelope becomes a real
  seek/loop-wrap discontinuity and advances the timing generation.
- The managed Carabiner helper remains a foreground child owned by the engine;
  normal Quit, unexpected UI disconnect and runtime drop terminate that exact
  child.

## Reason

On a fully occupied Pro DJ Link network, active metadata queries may be unable
to claim a player number and retry continuously. That work is unnecessary for
the USB-first Lumi architecture and must not compete with realtime lighting
output or place avoidable pressure on SoundSwitch.

Physical testing also showed that a delayed status frame could contain several
beats of legitimate elapsed progress. The old fixed `> 2 beats` rule treated
that as a seek, invalidated prepared work and re-anchored Link even though the
CDJ timeline had not changed. SoundSwitch then appeared to scrub or restart an
otherwise stable AutoLoop.

## Acceptance

- The Java bridge verification suite passes.
- Rust workspace, Pro DJ Link provider and Apple quality gates pass.
- Delayed and out-of-order status regressions prove that ordinary playing
  progress does not emit a seek, rewind the canonical beat or advance the
  transport generation.
- A real bundled-helper regression and two installed-app process checks prove
  cleanup after normal Quit and a forced UI exit; no Lumi engine or Carabiner
  process remains.
- Physical Player 1/SoundSwitch long-loop acceptance remains required before
  promotion; a device-only discovery without precise Beat packets is not
  accepted as timing evidence.
